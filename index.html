Odd One Out

Five party games for one phone and the people around you

Pass-and-play party games with no accounts, no internet and nothing to sign up for. One phone goes round the table; everything happens in the room.

The games

  • Different Word — everyone gets the same word, except one person.
  • Fib — everyone answers the same question, except one person who has to guess it.
  • The Suspects — hidden roles, private clues and an accusation each round.
  • Number Wavelength — everyone gets a number, one person gets a different one.
  • Devil's Advocate — everyone argues a side, and one person is arguing the wrong one.
